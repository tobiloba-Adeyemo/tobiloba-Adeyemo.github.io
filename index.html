Intro

Hi, I’m Tobiloba an Economics graduate with experience in data analytics and a strong interest in using data to solve business problems and support informed decision-making. I have developed practical skills in Microsoft Excel, SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, DAX and Power Query, with experience in data cleaning, analysis, reporting, data visualization and dashboard development. .

This portfolio showcases my practical data analytics projects across different business and analytical areas. My projects include analyzing sales performance and profitability, business costs and trends, customer retention and churn, and civic engagement data. Each project highlights a different stage of the data analytics process—from exploring and querying data to creating interactive dashboards and communicating insights that can support better business decisions.

Metrobank Customer Insights

Developed a customer transaction dashboard analyzing 5,000 transactions cross 800 accounts and $25M+ in transaction value. Revealed a near-even distribution across deposits, payments, transfers and withdrawals, while identifying POS as the highest-value channel and lower engagement across online and ATM channels.

Tools: Advanced Excel (Power Query, Power Pivot, Data Model, DAX, PivotTables, Data Visualization)

Workforce Overview Dashboard

An interactive workforce dashboard analyzing 311 employees, with 207 active employees and a 50.2% attrition rate. Revealed that Production accounts for 67% of the workforce (209 employees) and has the highest attrition with 83 departures, while providing insights into employee demographics, engagement, compensation and performance

Tools: Advanced Excel (Power Query, Power Pivot, Data Model, DAX, PivotTables, Data Visualization)

Meridian Retail Performance & Customer Analytics Dashboard

Created a 4-page interactive Power BI reporting solution providing a consolidated view of sales, profitability, customers, products, regional performance and discounting activities. Analyzed regional and category performance, discount-to-profit relationships and customer segment behavior to uncover the key factors influencing overall business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

Dashboards: Executive Summary | Regional & Category Performance | Discount vs Profit Analysis | Customer Segment Analysis

Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling, Interactive Data Visualization

Sales Performance Analysis — 2023 vs 2024

Created an interactive Excel dashboard comparing sales performance across 2023 and 2024, with a focus on YoY growth, revenue, sales volume, profitability and product-level performance. Analyzed changes in key performance indicators to identify top revenue drivers, shifts in product demand, and cost-to-profit trends, providing a clear view of sales growth and operational efficiency.

About

My journey into data analytics is rooted in a strong foundation in Economics. Studying market principles taught me to look beyond surface-level metrics to understand the fundamental drivers of a business, an analytical mindset that naturally evolved into a passion for data storytelling and strategic problem-solving. The reality of business analytics is that data is rarely clean and I thrive in navigating that complexity. Whether I am utilizing SQL and Python to clean and validate over 500,000 rows of complex inventory records or designing interactive reporting solutions in Power BI and Advanced Excel, my primary focus remains constant: transforming raw, fragmented information into intuitive, actionable insights that empower decision-makers.

Beyond technical execution, I value cross-functional collaboration, financial stewardship, and accountability. Taking on leadership responsibilities, such as managing financial duties and community initiatives as the Treasurer of the Financial Inclusion CDS Group under the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC), has reinforced my ability to communicate transparently, build trust and drive team objectives forward with precision. I approach my career with a mindset of continuous improvement, constantly refining my craft through structured training, active mentorship and the pursuit of advanced technical certifications. Ultimately, my goal is to bridge the gap between complex datasets and business strategy, bringing both technical rigor and a strong commercial acumen to every project I undertake.
